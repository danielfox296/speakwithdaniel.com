Skreened

Founder & art direction · Custom apparel · 2006–2017

I founded a custom-apparel marketplace and art-directed it for eleven years: the brand, the storefront, the campaigns, and the production floor they ran on. A mockup engine I designed staged every seller design on photographed garment templates, so each design, color, and product combination had its own search-built product image. The first $8M of sales came with near-zero ad spend.

The garment layer: photographed, retouched templates the engine composited seller art onto.

About

I studied Visual Communication Design at Ohio State, then spent eleven years inside a company where design and the production floor shared a wall. Most of what's here shipped: printed, poured, hung, worn, or streamed.

I work in Figma and the Adobe suite, I shoot and edit video, and I still kern things nobody asked me to kern.
